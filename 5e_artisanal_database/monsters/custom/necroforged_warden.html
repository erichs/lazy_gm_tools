Necroforged Warden

Medium Construct, Lawful Neutral

  • Armor Class: 19 (forged plate)
  • Hit Points: 85 (10d8 + 40)
  • Speed: 30 ft.
  • Initiative: +0 (10)
STAT SCORE MOD SAVE
STR 18 +4 +4
DEX 10 +0 +0
CON 18 +4 +7
INT 6 -2 -2
WIS 12 +1 +4
CHA 5 -3 -3
  • Immunities: Force, Poison, Psychic; Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Poisoned
  • Senses: blindsight 60 ft. (blind beyond this radius); Passive Perception 11
  • Languages: understands Kazadkhun and Dwarvish but can't speak
  • CR 5 (XP 1,800; PB +3)
  • Source: City of Arches

Traits

Drinks Force. Whenever the warden is subjected to Force damage, it takes no damage, and the next Greatsword hit it makes before the end of its next turn deals an extra 4 (1d8) Force damage.

Oath-Glyph. A lit glyph on the warden's breastplate is the oath that animates it. When the warden takes Radiant damage, the glyph gutters and the warden has Disadvantage on the next attack roll it makes before the end of its next turn.

Bound to the Door. The warden does not attack a creature of Kazadkhun blood who has touched a ward-glyph in its sight, nor any creature touching that one. It will not leave the hall and threshold it guards.

Empty Armor. When the warden drops to 0 Hit Points, the glyph goes dark and the armor falls apart, empty but for grey dust. Its glyph-plate can be pried loose. While the warden is Prone or Incapacitated, a creature within 5 feet can take an action to cut the plate free early (DC 15 Strength (Athletics) check); the warden is destroyed if it succeeds.

Actions

Multiattack. The warden makes two Greatsword attacks, against different targets if it can.

Greatsword. Melee Attack Roll: +7, reach 5 ft. 11 (2d6 + 4) Slashing damage plus 4 (1d8) Necrotic damage.

Reactions

Bar the Way. Trigger: A creature the warden can sense moves through a space within 5 feet of it. Response: The warden makes one Greatsword attack against that creature. On a hit, the creature's Speed becomes 0 until the start of its next turn.

Appearance. Heavy dwarf-forged plate scaled to human height, dull black, with gem-set rivets that glow faintly. There is no face beyond the visor. The crest and the name-plate on the gorget have been chiselled blank; the glyph on the breastplate is the only mark left.

What it is. The Nameless King's door-guard: a retainer who chose to stay at the post, sealed into the armor with the oath as the only thing still moving it. It does not speak, does not pursue past its threshold, and cannot be bargained with, only satisfied.

Homebrew for the Veilmar campaign. First met as the "Necroforged Guardian" in session 23 (AC 19, 60 HP, two swings at +6); this is the same creature scaled for a level 7 party.
